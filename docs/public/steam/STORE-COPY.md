# Last Singularity Steam Copy Draft

## Short Description

Surf an ASCII-dithered spacetime ocean, loot the last wrecks of a collapsing
universe, and extract before the final black hole keeps you.

## Long Description

Last Singularity is a top-down extraction roguelike about movement, pressure,
and dread. Your ship does not simply fly through space. It rides a simulated
fluid field warped by wells, stars, portals, wrecks, and stranger things that
push back as the universe runs out.

Every run is a fresh map. Read the flow, conserve delta-v, loot what you can,
and choose when to risk one more route toward a closing wormhole. Survive and
your cargo feeds the next launch. Collapse, and the universe keeps the rest.

## Feature Bullets

- Surf a Three.js ASCII-fluid renderer built around flow, distortion, bloom,
  scanlines, and high-contrast silhouettes.
- Play short extraction runs where movement fuel, cargo greed, portal timing,
  signal, and collapse pressure all compete for attention.
- Tune your hull and loadout between runs with persistent vault progression.
- Encounter scavengers, wells, portals, wreck fields, stars, and inhibitors
  that corrupt the UI and reshape how the field reads.
- Built first for keyboard/mouse and controller, with an explicit Steam Deck
  local-test path.
