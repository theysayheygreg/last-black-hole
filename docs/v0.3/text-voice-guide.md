# Last Singularity Text Voice Guide

## The failing instrument

The writing is what a failing instrument can still print: terse, calm under pressure, and exact about what it knows. It reports from the workbench rather than selling an imagined future. Dread arrives through omission and consequence, never a joke or a slogan.

## Rules contributors can apply

- Use short operational clauses. HUD labels: 1–4 words. Warnings: one clause, normally under 52 characters. Supporting copy: one sentence, normally under 90 characters.
- Default to lowercase for telemetry, labels, and supporting copy. Use uppercase only for terminal outcomes, section headers, and immediate warnings the instrument must surface.
- Name the observed state, then the available action. Do not invent certainty, odds, power, or an off-screen actor.
- Let empty state be quiet: `--`, `no recorded cycles`, and `no unusual telemetry` are stronger than filler.
- Say **aperture**, **confirm**, **abort**, **extracted**, **collapsed**, **residue**, and **echo** as defined in `theme-glossary.md`.
- Use semantic colors only when their role matters: cyan route/extraction, amber salvage/value, red immediate consequence, magenta Inhibitor/corruption/anomaly. Never use `gold` as a player-facing semantic color.
- Signal is a consequence. Never describe it as energy, currency, a purchase, or a desirable meter target.
- Extraction is not triumph. Report it as a confirmed state, then report what came through.
- Do not make the internal hull roster visible. Drifter and Breacher are the public hull names.

## Escalation

| State | Voice | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Nominal | low, factual | `route open` |
| Pending | exact and actionable | `aperture ready — confirm extraction` |
| Immediate | terse uppercase | `ROUTE CLOSED` |
| Terminal | outcome first | `COLLAPSED` |
| Inhibitor | broken only at the corruption layer | `signal breach` → bounded glyph damage, never unreadable controls |

Silence is a valid report. An unknown field is `--`; a missing history is `no recorded cycles`; a deliberate truncation ends at the available truth.

## Worked rewrites

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `surf the currents. escape the void.` | `read the current. find an aperture.` | Replaces generic heroic copy with actual verbs and a truthful extraction condition. |
| `the exits are already closing. move.` | `apertures are closing. choose a line.` | Keeps urgency without an arcade bark. |
| `civilizations fell like rain here. their wealth remains.` | `wrecks remain. exits do not.` | Avoids loot language and keeps the instrument terse. |
| `you made it through the aperture` | `aperture confirmed` | Extraction is reported, not celebrated. |
| `this is what the universe kept` | `telemetry retained` | A result screen reports the known record without claiming cosmic intent. |
| `cargo full — inventory to drop` | `cargo full — open inventory to drop` | Keeps the mechanic and removes ambiguity. |

## Public-workbench alignment

The public voice may use complete sentences, but stays evidence-led: name a real surface, describe the constraint, and leave claims smaller than the uncertainty. It does not call a candidate released, Deck-verified, multiplayer-ready, or final without the corresponding evidence. See `docs/public/TWITTER-POST-OPPORTUNITIES.md` for the public-copy boundary.
