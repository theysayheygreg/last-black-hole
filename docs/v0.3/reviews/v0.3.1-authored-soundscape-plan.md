# v0.3.1 Authored Soundscape Plan

**Status:** TODO in v0.3.1. Design and production work has not started.

**Scope correction (Greg, 2026-07-28):** this work belongs to the v0.3.1
feature line rather than the later v0.3.2 presentation-design milestone. The
current Hermes/Maestro contact-audio integration and afternoon RC remain useful
playtest checkpoints; they do not make v0.3.1 audio content-complete.

## v0.3.1 Baseline Correction

The v0.3.1 RC swarm repair is functional navigation plumbing, not final
audio-content acceptance. Timbre did not create authored WAV, OGG, or MP3
assets. The repair added procedural Web Audio oscillator tones and connected
runtime contacts for EXFIL, Vessel/Swarm, and Glitch, including spatial
gain/pan, priority, lifecycle, and fades.

Machine evidence may prove that those contacts route, prioritize, spatialize,
fade, and clean up correctly. It cannot prove that the game has an authored
soundscape, that the mix has the intended character, or that it sounds good on
Greg's target devices.

## v0.3.1 Outcome

Design and implement a larger authored-audio pass that gives Last Singularity
a deliberate sonic identity rather than treating procedural oscillator
coverage as finished content. Preserve the existing event, spatial, priority,
and lifecycle plumbing where it remains useful; replace or deepen its sound
sources based on listening results.

The pass must cover:

1. **Soundscape direction:** define the audible identity of quiet drift,
   movement, fabric/wells, salvage, EXFIL, Inhibitors, danger escalation,
   death, extraction, menus, and result states. Establish what remains
   procedural, what becomes authored source audio, and how both coexist.
2. **Asset inventory and coverage:** map every required continuous layer,
   one-shot, loop, transition, and intentional silence to its gameplay role,
   source owner, current placeholder, missing asset, and priority.
3. **Source creation:** create or record original source material and retain
   provenance, editable masters, runtime exports, format metadata, loop points,
   and revision ownership. Do not add WAV/OGG/MP3 files merely to satisfy a
   coverage count.
4. **Integration and mix:** bind approved assets through the existing audio
   router/mixer; tune spatial falloff, pan, priority, ducking, concurrency,
   transitions, loudness hierarchy, and lifecycle without moving gameplay
   truth into audio.
5. **Representative listening review:** review quiet traversal, high-speed
   movement, salvage, EXFIL approach/confirmation, each Inhibitor family,
   crowded late-match pressure, death, extraction, menus, and repeated-run
   fatigue. Compare at least one restrained and one bolder direction where
   taste remains unsettled.
6. **Device acceptance:** listen on headphones and ordinary speakers, then
   validate the packaged build on the physical Steam Deck for audibility,
   balance, distortion, fatigue, spatial legibility, suspend/resume, and
   real-device volume behavior.

## Evidence And Acceptance

Keep two ledgers:

- **Machine-verified:** asset presence and manifest closure, event-to-cue
  hookups, dedupe, priority/admission, spatial gain/pan, source counts,
  lifecycle/fades, loop seams, package inclusion, and performance.
- **Human accepted:** soundscape direction, timbre, emotional arc, mix,
  readability, fatigue, headphones/speakers balance, and physical Steam Deck
  behavior.

Automated evidence may block broken plumbing or packaging. It may not declare
audio-content taste accepted. Greg owns the representative listening verdict
and physical-device acceptance.

## Start Gate

Do not turn the active contact-audio RC repair into an unbounded sound-library
rewrite. After that exact candidate is packaged for playtest, continue on the
v0.3.1 feature line as a bounded audio vertical. Start with direction and a
coverage inventory, then commit representative authored slices independently;
do not wait for the entire library before putting the first real soundscape in
Greg's hands. v0.3.1 is not content-complete until Greg has heard and accepted
the representative mix, but that later listening gate does not invalidate the
intermediate RC used to test the new contact plumbing.
