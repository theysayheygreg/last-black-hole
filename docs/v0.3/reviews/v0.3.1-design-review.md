# v0.3.1 Design Review (running doc)

> Owner: Greg + Orrery. Started 2026-07-14. Living doc — updated as the
> review conversation continues. Scope: design-level issues in the v0.3 line,
> not code architecture (that's the 2026-07-04 deep review).

## Frame

v0.3's machine gates are green, but the design-expression debt is untouched:
zero commits since 2026-07-04 touch signal, the Inhibitor, or dread. Systems
are in excellent shape; expression (clock, signal ecology, dread arc, mastery
feedback) is unwired. v0.3.1 is the release that pays that down.

## The Five Tensions

Greg has agreed with 1–4. Item 5 is expanded below.

### 1. The RC gate measures the machine, not the game
Universe Is the Clock and Dread Over Difficulty have no expression surface and
no evidence gate. The build almost certainly fails "mute the HUD — can you
feel the urgency?" Collapse is sim-real but visually mute.

### 2. Signal is a meter, not an ecology
The consequence chain (loud act → something heard → something coming) is not
legible. Fix is wiring, not new systems: make scavenger attention attributable
to the player's own wake.

### 3. The Inhibitor is a mechanic, not a presence
Arrival mechanics exist (form indicator, warnings, haunts, June commits);
anticipation and aftermath don't. Magenta is reserved but nothing escalates
inside it.

### 4. Mastery has nowhere to compound
No surface teaches or rewards reading the fluid (crest brightening, slingshot
payoff, wave tells). Run-to-run improvement is loot RNG and route memory, not
fluid literacy.

### 5. The pressure meter — a debug readout became product truth (EXPANDED)

**The object:** Inhibitor pressure integrates signal, elapsed time, and well
growth. It is the convergence point of pillars 3, 4, and 5 — one scalar
carrying the game's dramatic thesis.

**The problem:** it renders as `building // 42%` — an edge meter visible from
5% pressure onward. Precision kills dread: players will learn "extract by
85%" and the clock becomes a resource ceiling. Pillar 3 collapses into meter
management; pillar 5 becomes the difficulty timer it was written against.

**How it happened:** sim needed a scalar → tests needed to observe it → solo
HUD shipped a widget → v0.4 P3 needed a *shared* world read and the scalar
was the only tap → now tests and the crew rail bind to it. Nobody chose this.
The plan's own tell: the meter is "explicitly labeled world pressure" —
when you must caption a feeling, the feeling isn't there.

**Diagnosis — missing channel, not extra widget:** the presentation layer
receives gameplay data but no dramatic data. The fabric never hears about
pressure. Kill the meter without building the channel and another meter grows
back. Irony: the fabric is already the perfect shared surface (driven by
authoritative state) — four players watching the same dying sky get
synchronized dread for free.

**Direction:**
- Keep the scalar in sim and protocol. Change consumers.
- `pressureFrac` drives **banded** fabric tells: palette temperature drains,
  glyph density decays, drone detunes at thresholds. Weather fronts, not
  progress bars.
- Form transitions become world events (hooks exist:
  `publishEvent("inhibitor.form")`): fabric shudder, corruption bloom, audio
  register turn.
- Extend the existing HUD text-corruption grammar (`inhibitorTextAmount`,
  proximity-scaled — good bones) into the fabric itself.
- Edge meter demotes to accessibility/debug once environmental reads pass.
- v0.4 crew rail keeps crew truth (seats/links/life — legitimately UI).
  World truth moves to the world.

**Falsifiable gate:** screenshots at pressure 0.2 / 0.5 / 0.9, HUD hidden;
reviewer must be able to order them. Add to RC in the spirit of the magenta
fixture checks.

## Red Flags Ledger

- **0.000x tuning constants** (Greg): `pressureFromTime: 0.0005/s` in
  `INHIBITOR_CONFIG`. Constants that small mean the units are wrong, the
  normalization is hiding elsewhere, or the knob does nothing a playtester
  can feel. Audit all sub-0.01 modifiers in sim config for honest units
  (e.g. express as time-to-threshold: "empty-handed run fills pressure in
  ~33 min" is tunable; 0.0005 is not).

## Sequencing Lean

Collapse expression first (item 5/1): it is the channel pillars 3 and 5 speak
through, and every week it doesn't exist, v0.4 hardens another UI contract
around its absence. Signal ecology legibility second.
