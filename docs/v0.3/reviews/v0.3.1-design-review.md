## Greg's Frame (2026-07-14)

Ideal outcomes and problem areas, verbatim intent:

1. **Systems are scaffolded, not fulfilled.** Individual systems are
   implemented but don't fulfill design intent. Each likely needs an explicit
   **v2 goal** to iterate on and measure success against.
2. **Examples:** NPCs/AI feel like stubs, not features. Slingshot isn't
   usable or well telegraphed — should feel like a grappling hook.
   Inhibitors read as tapestry, not threat. Plenty more.
3. **Design docs are stale.** Originals need updating, archiving, or
   versioning. What was the original goal? Did we do it? Sometimes yes,
   sometimes no. Old docs are **not sacrosanct**.
4. **Tunables and math are loose.** Hard to tell if player-usable skill does
   anything against the larger sim physics. Applies to anything with a
   number attached. (See Red Flags Ledger.)

The five tensions below remain valid inputs, but the pass is broader: audit
every system against intent, set v2 goals, and version the doc truth.

## Inputs: The Five Tensions (Orrery, 2026-07-14)

Greg agrees with 1–4; 5 expanded.

1. **RC gate measures the machine, not the game.** Clock and Dread pillars
   have no expression surface and no evidence gate. Build likely fails
   "mute the HUD — can you feel the urgency?"
2. **Signal is a meter, not an ecology.** Consequence chain (loud act →
   heard → coming) is not legible. Wiring, not new systems.
3. **Inhibitor is a mechanic, not a presence.** Arrival exists; anticipation
   and aftermath don't. Magenta is reserved but nothing escalates in it.
4. **Mastery has nowhere to compound.** Nothing teaches/rewards reading the
   fluid; improvement is loot RNG and route memory.
5. **Pressure meter: debug readout became product truth.** Inhibitor
   pressure (signal + time + well growth) is the convergence of pillars 3/4/5
   and renders as `building // 42%`. Precision kills dread; players will
   optimize the meter. Root cause: no dramatic-data channel to the fabric,
   so every surface (solo HUD, v0.4 crew rail) taps UI. Direction: banded
   fabric tells (palette drain, glyph decay, drone detune), form transitions
   as world events, extend the existing corruption grammar into the fabric,
   demote the meter. Falsifiable gate: order HUD-less screenshots at
   pressure 0.2/0.5/0.9.

## Red Flags Ledger

- **0.000x tuning constants** (Greg): e.g. `pressureFromTime: 0.0005/s`.
  Wrong units, hidden normalization, or an unfeelable knob. Audit all
  sub-0.01 sim constants; re-express in perceivable units (time-to-threshold,
  per-run totals).
- **Skill-vs-physics legibility** (Greg): player-usable skill effects are
  indistinguishable from ambient sim forces. Candidate rule: any player
  ability must move an observable outcome by a felt margin, provable by an
  A/B probe.
