# v0.3.1 Encounter Generation — Catalog, Spawn Tables, Placement Logic

> Design exercise 2026-07-14 (Greg + Orrery). Companion to the review's
> S24. Sources: `docs/reference/OPEN-WORLD-DENSITY-RESEARCH.md`,
> `STELLARIS-PVE-AI-RESEARCH.md`, extraction research (inbound).
> Map bounds ruling: **5×5 wu minimum to 25×25 wu maximum** (3×3 Shallows
> grows to the floor). Cell = 1×1 wu for placement math.

## Ratings model

- **Size** = footprint reservation: S=1 cell, M=2×2, L=3×3, XL=5×5
  (plus per-encounter exclusion halo).
- **Difficulty D0–D5** = combat threat and/or physics challenge. Physics
  counts as difficulty — a D3 can have zero enemies.
- **Loot L0–L5.** Scaling law: expected value tracks D at the same
  location (risk-reward must be geographically readable). L may exceed D
  only via **locks** (key needed) or **physics gates** (the challenge is
  the guard). Guarantee slots (always-present items, exfil keys) exist
  only in L/XL entries and locked S/M vaults.
- **Challenge axis** tags: ⚔ combat, ⚛ physics, ◎ ecology/social,
  ∅ negative space, 🔑 knowledge/lock.
- Entries marked **[W1-gate]** depend on proven entity locomotion
  (Greg's execution caveat) and stay table-inactive until Wave 1 lands.

## Map Spawn Tables (5×5 → 25×25)

Budget rules:
- **Footprint budget:** total encounter cells ≤ 25–30% of map area. The
  rest is connective sea, free terrain, and authored voids.
- **Difficulty budget:** Σ(D × size-weight) within a per-map band; band
  scales with map tier, and hard maps also have a difficulty FLOOR.
- **Neighborhood cap:** sum of D in any 5×5 window ≤ band max (no
  accidental double-stacked death zones — free wells count).
- **Guarantee slots:** every map carries #46 (Singularity) plus its
  tier's fixed counts; one-of-Z slots surface in briefing only as an
  anomalous reading.

| Map size | Cells | S | M | L | XL (besides #46) | One-of-Z | D range | Authored voids |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5×5 (floor/teaching) | 25 | 3–4 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 (S/M list) | D0–2 | 1 small |
| 10×10 | 100 | 6–8 | 3–4 | 1–2 | 0–1 | 1 | D0–3 | 1–2 |
| 15×15 | 225 | 10–12 | 5–6 | 2–3 | 1 | 1–2 | D1–4, floor D2 | 2 |
| 20×20 | 400 | 13–16 | 7–8 | 3–4 | 1–2 | 2 | D1–5, floor D2 | 2–3 |
| 25×25 (max) | 625 | 16–20 | 8–10 | 4–5 | 2 | 2 + 1 rumor | D2–5, floor D3 | 3+ |

Per Greg's consistency rule: a given MAP fixes its counts exactly
("map 1 always has 4×X, 2×Y, 1-of-Z") within these bands, and each map
tier pulls from a different, clearly-known difficulty table. Players
learn the table; the seed decides casting and placement.

Spacing rules (pending the travel-time probe for real numbers):
- From any route point, a flag-tier read within ~30 s of travel
  (Tsushima rule), tuned via sensor radius as much as placement.
- Weenies sensable map-wide; the Singularity always.
- View/sensor distance capped well below wrap distance.
- Every authored void ≥ a stated crossing time; patrols may cross
  voids (periodic dread), static hostiles may not sit in them.

## Placement: Order of Operations

WFC-flavored (below), but the collapse PRIORITY encodes this order and
its reasons:

1. **Global frame.** Seed → map size/tier table → field effect (S9b) →
   difficulty/loot budgets → anisotropy direction → Conductor skeleton
   (phases, portal windows). Field resolves FIRST because it modifies
   the placement constraints themselves (e.g. Dark Nebula shrinks
   sensor range → tightens the 30-second spacing rule).
2. **The Singularity (#46).** The anchor. Its position defines the risk
   gradient and, with the Dipole when drawn, the map's "north."
   Everything else places relative to it.
3. **Exfil frame (S15).** Portal sites + guaranteed final exfil in
   distance bands from the anchor, with clearance halos. BEFORE
   encounters, because exfil geometry must be deterministic and fair —
   if placed after, set pieces wall off doors or swallow them.
4. **XL then L set pieces** (incl. one-of-Z). Big-first bin packing
   while space is open. Patrol circuits (#47) reserve corridors now.
   Conflict rule: a patrol corridor may cross voids but must not
   sterilize the only route between two exfils.
5. **Free wells / stars / planetoids** (connective gravity terrain).
   After set pieces so corridors between them get deliberate slingshot
   chains (spacing ≈ chain reachability). Neighborhood-D cap counts
   these.
6. **Seeded sea.** Swell trains + tide pools derive AFTER all masses
   exist (currents come from wells) but before small placements that
   want surf spots. Storm/shear encounters (#40) register their zones
   here.
7. **M/S encounters.** Fill to the density rhythm: S-tier biases along
   sightlines between L/XL (breadcrumb chaining, #2 literally points).
   Locks and keys place in DIFFERENT regions, key earlier in the risk
   gradient than its lock (inverted deliberately only at D5).
8. **Ambient populations.** Free fauna/scavengers via threshold fields
   + nurseries — last of the spatial passes because nurseries must
   avoid hostile nests and voids.
9. **Loot pass.** Guarantees first (set-piece items, keys), then local
   tables scaled by the D actually present after all stacking.
10. **Conductor wiring.** Register encounter timers (pulsar beats,
    patrols, revivals, #42/#50 wave origins); verify offset guard
    against phases and portal windows.
11. **Validation & repair.** Constraint re-check; local repair or
    reseed on contradiction (below).

Named conflicts this order resolves:
- exfils vs set-piece footprints → exfils first + clearance halos, with
  repair pass for small maps;
- patrol corridors vs voids vs density rule → patrols-may-cross-voids
  rule; a void with a periodic leviathan is a feature;
- current lanes vs extraction → final exfil never directly downstream
  of a jet;
- key/lock geography → cross-region rule above;
- difficulty double-stacking → neighborhood cap counts free terrain.

## Wave-Function-Collapse Rule

Treat the map as coarse cells, each holding a superposition of allowed
contents (encounter classes, free terrain, void, sea-only). Authoring
surface = the **adjacency rule table** + collapse priors:

- Constraints propagate: "hostile nest never adjacent to nursery,"
  "void must border at least one sightline to a flag," "D gradient
  trends radial from the anchor," "exfil halo excludes D≥3,"
  neighborhood-D cap, footprint reservations.
- Collapse order = the priority list above (anchor and exfils are
  pre-collapsed observations; then lowest-entropy cells first).
- Seeded RNG (dedicated `rng-stream` streams per pass — placement
  stream, casting stream, loot stream — so adding a draw in one pass
  never shifts another) picks among surviving options.
- Contradiction → local repair (re-open the smallest offending
  neighborhood) before full reseed; bounded retries; the map hash
  records repair count for tuning telemetry.

WFC gives local variety under global coherence — the order of
operations becomes priors rather than a brittle sequence, and every
conflict above is an adjacency rule instead of special-case code.

## Uniqueness Guarantee

- Same seed → same map (S8a non-negotiable, unchanged).
- Across matches: uniqueness levers multiply — table casting (which
  of Z, which variants), per-encounter variant rolls (layout template
  rotation/mirror/jitter within schema), WFC placement space, field
  effect, sea seed, Conductor offsets.
- **Enforced, not hoped:** each generated map produces a digest (cast +
  placement neighborhood hash). The server keeps the last N digests per
  map tier and rerolls the seed on collision or near-collision. Recent
  runs can never repeat; distant echoes are astronomically unlikely.
- Chronicle stores the digest with the run — "the universe where X
  happened" is literally addressable (S19).

## Keys, Staged Unlocks, and Match Escalation (folded 2026-07-14)

Extraction research applied as mechanics, not notes.

### The key economy (three persistence tiers, Marathon model)

| Tier | Lifetime | LBH form | Sources | Sinks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Persistent keys** | Across runs (Greg decision pending) | Named physical keys in cargo/vault | #30 Drowned Convoy guarantee, #36 Station interiors, rare wreck reveals (S14 deep slots) | Named locked rooms in #36/#39/#48; carrying one to its lock is a run plan |
| **In-run keys** | This match only | Keycards/fragments | #13 Vault Splinter pairs, #35 Sunken Vault, scav drops, event rewards | Locked exfils (S15), #48 stage doors; die with you (cargo loss makes keys stakes) |
| **Per-run clearance** | This match, earned meter | Squad-shared clearance 0→N | Terminal interacts at set pieces, apex kills, event completions | Gates #48's deeper stages and #50's core; NEVER gates the guaranteed final exfil (S15 determinism overrides the Cryo model here) |

Rules:
- **The hint layer is content:** #10 Echo Buoys and chronicle fragments
  reveal where this match's keys/locks landed — knowledge objects, not
  flavor.
- **Every unlock is LOUD** (Marathon gold-key rule): opening any locked
  room/vault/exfil emits a world-visible+audible event. Knowledge-gated
  wealth paints a target; couples the S13 greed trigger to the rival
  dinner bell.
- Locks and their keys place in different regions (existing WFC rule);
  hint buoys bias placement along routes between them.

### Staged unlocks (the Cryo Archive lesson, sized for LBH)

- **#48 Megastructure Arc is the staged flagship:** its ring fragments
  are sequential stages — each core opened (clearance + in-run keys)
  unlocks the next, escalating D per stage, capstone requires items
  from prior stages. A multi-run community learning curve on a set
  piece that moves every match.
- **#50 The Roil stages with the Inhibitor clock:** its depth is
  clearance-gated AND phase-gated — the richest core is only reachable
  late, when severity waves are worst. The Roil is the spatial
  expression of the match clock.
- **#36 Wrecked Station** gets a two-stage interior: open hull (free),
  sealed section (in-run key), captain's vault (persistent key —
  the "important item" guarantee lives at whichever stage the map tier
  allows).
- Staging rule: stages escalate D by +1 each and loot per the L-law;
  every stage-open is loud; abandoning mid-chain leaves it open for
  rivals (staged progress is world state, not private state).

### Match escalation (Conductor events as dinner bells)

Events exist to re-concentrate dispersed players and reset decayed
pressure (the Watkins law: static PvE gets solved; escalation needs
live tunables + telemetry from day one).

| Event | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Boss dice** | Per-match roll (~30%, Tarkov model) on eligible L/XL entries | Apex entity present or not — the table is known, the presence is dice |
| **Anomaly Surge** | Conductor schedule, random eligible encounter | Carryable anomaly spawns; extracting it = big payout; carrying it is loud and slow |
| **Lockdown** | Conductor schedule | Hazard field over one encounter; survives = high-tier cache; reshapes usable space temporarily |
| **Guarded exfil** | Any exfil activation (S15) | Activation spawns a hostile response wave — extraction generates its own pressure |
| **Convergence** | Main timer end (S15 final exfil) | All optional doors close; every survivor funnels to the guaranteed final — the endgame is a scene by construction |
| **Wave origins** | #42/#50 present | Severity waves emanate from places; destroying #42 visibly thins them (legible de-escalation) |

Escalation pairing rule (ARC conditions, adopted verbatim): any event
or field that raises difficulty raises reward with it — never
independently.

### The Response Ecology (Inhibitor + AI players as escalation actors)

Marathon's UESC model, split across LBH's two response forces. The
Conductor keeps a **response ledger** — every escalation-worthy act
(loud unlock, exfil activation, stage-open, anomaly pickup, apex kill)
is an entry that dispatches responses from two independent actors:

- **The Inhibitor is the world's immune system (PvE response).**
  Activation responses draw from a phase-indexed response table: the
  same guarded-exfil activation that summons a tier-1 nuisance wave in
  phase 1 brings hunters in phase 3. Severity waves already emanate
  from places (#42/#50); the ledger gives them *reasons* — responses
  route toward the act that earned them. Escalation is therefore both
  scheduled (phases) and provoked (your acts), and the two multiply:
  late-match greed is the most expensive greed.
- **AI players are the rival response (PvP-shaped).** No special
  escalation AI and no cheating: they receive exactly the
  world-visible events humans receive (the loud-unlock broadcast, the
  exfil beam, the anomaly klaxon — symmetric information, systems-peer
  doctrine). Their escalation arc emerges from the clock itself:
  they're on the same stakes, so they loot early, contest mid, and
  MUST push exfils before Convergence — collisions at doors and vaults
  intensify automatically as the timer runs down, no scripting.
  **[W1-gate]** for implementation; the contract is set now.
- **Staged unlocks feed both:** each stage-open on #36/#48/#50 writes a
  ledger entry — deeper stages draw phase-scaled Inhibitor responses
  AND advertise to rivals. The capstone of a staged chain late in a
  match should be the most contested moment the map can produce.

One invariant ties it together: **responses are always attributable.**
A wave that arrives has a visible origin and a visible cause (the
telegraph registry covers response dispatch too). The player who gets
swarmed after cracking a vault in phase 3 should never wonder why.

## Extraction-Research Calibration (folded 2026-07-14)

Full report: `docs/reference/EXTRACTION-MAP-DESIGN-RESEARCH.md`.

- **The genre law and LBH's deliberate inversion.** All three games:
  geometry sacred, contents dice. LBH inverts — set pieces move — so the
  mastery substrate must be carried entirely by the stable language:
  exact per-map table counts, learnable encounter INTERNALS (each entry
  ships stable variants, not internal randomization), physics rules,
  Conductor grammar. If encounter internals churn, LBH has no mastery
  substrate at all. Rule: **randomize position and casting, never
  structure and theme.**
- **Marathon validates and extends S15:** Final Exfil as designed
  convergence (closing all other doors forces every survivor toward one
  point — the endgame is a scene by construction); used/optional exfils
  may rotate; activation spawning PvE pressure (guarded exfils) pairs
  with our manual-activation exposure window.
- **Locked loot is loud** (Marathon's gold-key audio broadcast):
  opening a vault/locked exfil should emit a world-visible/audible
  event — knowledge-gated wealth paints a target. Couples the S13 greed
  trigger to the rival dinner bell with zero new systems.
- **ARC Map Conditions validate S9b fields**, including the pairing
  rule: conditions raise difficulty AND loot together — risk and reward
  move as a pair, never independently.
- **PvE pressure decays as skill compounds** (Watkins, confirmed):
  severity-wave escalation must have live tunables and telemetry from
  day one; static pressure gets solved.
- **Events are dinner bells:** Conductor events should re-concentrate
  dispersed players mid-match, not just add danger.
- **Open Greg decision — key persistence tiers.** Marathon runs three:
  persistent keys (across runs) / in-run keycards / per-run clearance.
  S15 currently implies in-run only. Persistent keys would give the
  chronicle-era knowledge economy a physical form.
