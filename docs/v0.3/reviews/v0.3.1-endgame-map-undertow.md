## Identity

**The Undertow** — the throat of the watershed. Everything the dying
galaxy lost washes down through here on its way to the Drain. 25×25 wu,
endgame tier (D2–5, floor D3), current-defined geography: one great
S-curve of flow (the **Throat**) from the Wellspring (NW) to the Drain
(SE), with slack-water rims and violent confluences.

**The line that sells it: the way out is against the fall.** Every
optional exfil is a cross-stream commit; the guaranteed final exfil is
the Wellspring itself — the climb upstream while everything else
accelerates down.

## Flow Geography (why things are where they are)

- **Spawns (s1–s7 + one flex):** slack-water rim, upstream halves
  favored; `minFromOtherPlayers` ≥ 8 cells, `minFromObjective` ≥ 5.
  You start where the current is quiet and the debris is thin.
- **Loot lives at confluences** because that's where the sea PUTS
  drifting material — the watershed is the diegetic loot table.
- **Exfil pool (x1–x6):** cross-stream placements; two active at a
  time, drawn per interval, exhausted on use (Marathon-corrected S15).
  x3 is the **locked exfil** (Ark vault key). None sit downstream of
  the Throat (never a drift-out).
- **Final exfil: the Wellspring.** Guaranteed, fires at main-timer end.
  The last two minutes of any full run are an upstream climb against an
  accelerating current, past everyone else's ambitions.

## Keys, Stages, Clearance (the Cryo-complexity chain)

1. **Echo buoys (3)** each reveal one pairing this match: which shoal
   holds a splinter key, which exfil is locked, where the Convoy is.
2. **Drowned Convoy** carries the **Tollgate key** (in-run).
3. **Tollgate Station:** open hull free → sealed section (Tollgate key)
   → contains **Ark stage-key A** + a clearance terminal.
4. **The Ark, 5 stages:** A opens fragment 1; terminals at fragments
   1–3 build per-pilot clearance; fragments escalate D+1 each;
   **capstone** needs stage items from 1+3 AND phase ≥2 — guarantee:
   the map's important item + x3's exfil key.
5. **The Roil core:** clearance 3 + phase 3 — the greed-endgame:
   richest field, worst weather, longest climb home.
Every unlock is LOUD (world event; rivals and responses hear it).
Abandoned progress stays open — stages are world state.

## Why this map is endgame-complex (the Outpost/Cryo test)

Multi-POI key routes (buoy → convoy → station → ark), a staged
multi-run learning curve (Ark), per-run clearance, a phase-gated
optional superbowl (Roil core), rotating exfil pressure, one locked
exfil, a converging finale with built-in drama (upstream climb), and a
moving M-encounter (Convoy) threading the map's main artery. A veteran
knows the table cold and still cannot know THIS match's arrangement.

# Theme, Narrative, Visual Concepts

## Theme

The Undertow is **the drowned dockyard of the last exodus**. A
civilization (the Meridians) saw the fall coming and tried to build
their way out — the Ark is their unfinished escape ring, abandoned
mid-weld. The map is what the current has done with their ambition
since: sorted it, drowned it, and dragged it slowly toward the Drain.

## Narrative in geography (no codex required)

- **Spawn rims:** the first refugee camps — thin debris, old and picked
  over. You arrive where they arrived.
- **Tollgate Station:** the customs post that taxed the exodus. Its
  sealed section still holds what it confiscated.
- **The Ark:** five fragments of a ring that was going to carry
  everyone. The capstone vault holds what they considered worth saving
  when they knew they weren't leaving.
- **Graveyard Spiral:** the ships that waited too long, still queuing
  for a departure that never came, on a conveyor into the dark.
- **The Roil:** where the Inhibitor first breached — the reason the
  exodus failed. Corruption has held the high ground ever since.
- **The Bell:** their lighthouse and clock, still keeping time for
  nobody. Its pulse is the map's heartbeat and the player's tempo.
- **The Wellspring:** the anomaly they were trying to reach — the one
  thing in the sky that pushes back. It is the only way out, and it was
  always the only way out.

## Visual concepts

- **Palette:** abyssal indigo fabric; bone-white glyph currents; salvage
  gold glints; route cyan (portals/exfils); magenta strictly the Roil
  (discipline holds). The Drain is a **rim-glow horizon** — a sunken
  sun along the SE quadrant, light bending wrong at its edge
  (Interstellar treatment at map scale).
- **The current is the art direction:** glyph "grain" aligns with flow
  everywhere — the fabric IS the map. Reading the sea and reading the
  screen are the same act.
- **The Ark silhouette** reads as giant broken letters of a dead
  alphabet — set-piece-as-typography, on-brand for an ASCII game.
- **The Wellspring** is the only place glyphs *rise*: a pale upwelling
  column, the single vertical hope-read on a map that leans down-right.
- **Register by depth:** ambient drone deepens downstream; the Bell's
  pulse rides above it; the Roil detunes everything it touches.
